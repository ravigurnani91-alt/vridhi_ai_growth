KATHMANDU, BEYOND THE POSTCARDS · FIELD GUIDE

Pharping's Sacred Caves: The Stories Behind Asura Cave

Pharping sits on the southern rim of the Kathmandu Valley. Nepal Tourism Board describes its monasteries, Vajrayogini temple and a cave associated with the Buddhist master Padmasambhava.

VRIDHI EDITORIAL TEAM · 23 NOVEMBER 2026 · 4 MIN READ

The story behind the place

The cave is important within Tibetan Buddhist pilgrimage traditions. Stories connect Padmasambhava with meditation at the site; these are religious traditions and should be presented as such, not as independently verified archaeological events. The wider Pharping area brings together Buddhist and Hindu sacred places, making it a revealing stop beyond central Kathmandu.

How to experience it

Pharping is commonly approached from Kathmandu by road. Allow time for the hill setting and for visiting respectfully. Wear footwear suitable for steps and uneven paths, dress modestly at religious sites, and check local access before travelling. Do not touch sacred objects or enter restricted areas.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel details, access, opening arrangements, fees and local conditions can change. Confirm directly with local authorities or site caretakers before travelling. This guide is an editorial starting point, not a live access or booking service.

A question travellers ask

What is Pharping known for?

It is known for Buddhist pilgrimage sites, monasteries, caves associated with Padmasambhava traditions and the Vajrayogini temple.
