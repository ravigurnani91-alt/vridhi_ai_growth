KATHMANDU, BEYOND THE POSTCARDS · FIELD GUIDE

Sankhu and Vajrayogini: The Forest Shrine Above an Ancient Town

Sankhu is a historic settlement northeast of Kathmandu. Nepal's Department of Archaeology lists Vajrayogini and the early settlement of Sankhu on the country's World Heritage Tentative List.

VRIDHI EDITORIAL TEAM · 23 NOVEMBER 2026 · FIELD GUIDE

The story behind the place

The temple and settlement belong to a wider cultural landscape, not an isolated photo stop. The Department of Archaeology's tentative-list entry recognises the heritage significance of Vajrayogini and Sankhu. A tentative listing is not the same as inscription on the UNESCO World Heritage List; that distinction matters when describing the site.

How to experience it

Plan this as a half-day or longer outing from Kathmandu, allowing time for the approach and the historic town. The temple route may involve steps and uneven paths. Confirm local access, festival arrangements and transport before departure, and follow temple etiquette.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel details, access, opening arrangements, fees and local conditions can change. Confirm directly with local authorities or site caretakers before travelling. This guide is an editorial starting point, not a live access or booking service.

A question travellers ask

Is Vajrayogini Temple a UNESCO World Heritage Site?

The Vajrayogini and early settlement of Sankhu is on Nepal's Tentative List; it is not one of the seven monument zones inscribed within the Kathmandu Valley World Heritage property.
