QUICK ANSWERS

Before you go

What are some lesser-known places to visit in Kathmandu?

Itum Bahal, Pharping, Kirtipur, Chobhar, Sankhu and Bungamati offer a different perspective on the Kathmandu Valley beyond its best-known monuments.

How many days do you need?

Allow two or three days beyond the main city highlights to explore several outer-valley places without rushing.

Are all these sites UNESCO World Heritage Sites?

No. The Kathmandu Valley's inscribed property includes seven monument zones. Other places in this collection are outside those zones or are on Nepal's Tentative List.
