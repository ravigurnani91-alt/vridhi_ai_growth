Kirtipur: The Old Newar Town Above Kathmandu

Kirtipur is an old Newar town on a ridge southwest of Kathmandu, known for brick streets, tiled roofs, temple squares and a strong local identity.

VRIDHI EDITORIAL TEAM · 23 NOVEMBER 2026 · FIELD GUIDE

The story behind the place

Kirtipur is more than a viewpoint over the capital. Its urban fabric preserves traditional Newar houses and public religious spaces. Nepal Tourism Board highlights Bagh Bhairab Temple and Chilamchu Stupa, and notes the town's traditional festivals. The town also carries a difficult history connected with the Gorkha conquest; tell that history through credible historical sources and local voices rather than turning it into a simple legend.

How to experience it

Explore on foot, leaving time for temple squares and a meal at a local Newari eatery. Streets can be steep and uneven. Ask before photographing residents, homes or ceremonies, and check festival dates locally because the calendar changes year to year.

BEFORE YOU GO

Travel details, access, opening arrangements, fees and local conditions can change. Confirm directly with local authorities or site caretakers before travelling. This guide is an editorial starting point, not a live access or booking service.

A question travellers ask

What can you do in Kirtipur?

Walk the old town, visit Bagh Bhairab Temple and Chilamchu Stupa, learn about Newar culture and try local food.
