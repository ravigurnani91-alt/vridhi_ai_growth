Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit Vridhi and how analytics and advertising technologies may work.

EFFECTIVE DATE · 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Who operates this website

Vridhi operates the website at vridhi.ink as an independent knowledge, discovery and publishing platform.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting and security systems may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, requested URL and request time to deliver the site, protect it and diagnose errors. Server logs are handled by the hosting provider under its own terms.

Analytics

Vridhi uses Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website use, such as page views, traffic sources, device categories and interactions. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies and process online identifiers and device information. Analytics data is used to understand and improve the publication; it is not intended to identify readers by name.

For information about Google's data practices and available controls, consult Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics privacy information.

Advertising and cookies

Vridhi has applied to use Google AdSense. If advertising is approved and enabled, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise ads, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements and Google policies. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use information about visits to this and other websites to provide advertising services.

Readers can review or change Google's advertising personalisation choices at Google My Ad Center. Additional industry opt-out information may be available at YourAdChoices. Browser settings can also be used to manage or delete cookies, although some site functions may be affected.

Vridhi does not control cookies or data processing performed by third-party advertising, analytics, hosting or external-link providers. Their own privacy notices apply to those services.

Information you choose to provide

If Vridhi later offers subscriptions, surveys, comments or other interactive services, the information requested and its purpose will be explained at the point of collection. Do not send sensitive personal information through general website channels.

Children

Vridhi is a general-audience publication and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has submitted personal information should contact the site operator through the published contact channel.

Contact and privacy requests

For privacy questions, data-related requests or concerns about this policy, contact the Vridhi editorial team at ravi.gurnani91@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when site features, analytics or advertising arrangements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when a material update is made.
