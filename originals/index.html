Publishing rule: Vridhi Originals is original-work-only. It is not an open third-party author marketplace and does not promise publication to outside manuscript submissions.

QUALITY BEFORE QUANTITY

Make fewer things. Make them worth owning.

Each title should have a clear reader, a distinct promise, original value, appropriate review, a production plan and a reason to exist. We will validate demand before investing in printing, distribution or paid tools.
