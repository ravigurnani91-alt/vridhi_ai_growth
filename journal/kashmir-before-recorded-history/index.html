Kashmir:
Before Recorded History.

Before the cities, before the chronicles, before the names we recognise—what stories does this valley hold? Travel from the Satisar legend and the people of Burzahom to Buddhist scholars, mountain temples, philosophers and the writer who tried to record it all.
