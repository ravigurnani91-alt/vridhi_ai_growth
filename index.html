Why do some villages disappear while nearby towns keep growing?

Migration, jobs, water, transport, public services and changing livelihoods can all play a role. The reasons differ from place to place.

Can a city run out of groundwater even after a good monsoon?

Yes. Recharge depends on geology, land cover, drainage, extraction and how much rain actually enters the aquifer—not rainfall totals alone.

How do archaeologists distinguish a historical fact from a popular legend?

They compare material evidence, inscriptions, dating methods, written records and the context in which stories were passed down.
