OUR FIRST FIELD PROJECT · UDAIPUR

Understand a place.
Help it thrive.

Vridhi Earth will begin by bringing together local reporting, public information, research and community observations to build a clearer picture of environmental issues in Udaipur and its district.

  • Lakes, wetlands and water quality
  • Waste, sewage and urban pollution
  • Aravalli ecology and biodiversity
  • Climate resilience and civic action
