# Vridhi: Zero-Cost Revenue Execution Plan

**Operating rule:** ₹0 incremental spend. Do not create paid projects, subscriptions, API credits, ads, domains, hosting upgrades, or paid automation. Stop before any action that could create a charge.

## Business objective
Build a useful, trusted publication and intelligence service that earns revenue. The long-term ambition is 10,000 daily active users, but this is a target, not a forecast or guarantee. Track daily active users separately from pageviews, reach, and monthly users.

## Strategic pilot
Start with Udaipur environmental intelligence. Cover:
- Lakes and water quality
- Sewage, drainage, and urban infrastructure
- Waste, landfill fires, and collection
- Air quality
- Aravalli, biodiversity, and wetlands
- Urban development and climate resilience
- Environmental education and practical civic information

Every item must record publication/event date, location, summary, impact, source URL, evidence notes, and verification status. Separate verified facts, allegations, estimates, and opinion. Give subjects a fair opportunity to respond before publishing serious allegations. Do not publish unverified claims as fact.

## First revenue products (sell before building complex tech)
1. **Local sponsor package:** clearly labelled sponsor placement in a weekly local briefing and related social distribution. No sponsor influence over editorial conclusions.
2. **Paid research brief:** commissioned, source-linked environmental / local-market intelligence for businesses, institutions, hospitality operators, real-estate stakeholders, and civic organisations. Define scope and fee before work begins.
3. **Newsletter sponsorship:** only after a real opted-in subscriber base exists.

Do not count leads, verbal interest, or proposals as revenue. Report collected revenue separately from pipeline.

## Zero-cost operating stack
- Existing GitHub repository and GitHub Actions, within free limits.
- Existing Metricool access, within current plan limits.
- Google Analytics and Search Console, if already available at no cost.
- Existing Notion access, within current plan limits.
- Free web research and manual/assisted editorial production.
- No paid LLM API dependency. No Supabase project required for the initial static pilot; use a version-controlled content workflow until a genuine need is proven.

## 30-day execution sequence
### Days 1–3: protect and measure
- Preserve current site and confirm the production deployment before changing anything.
- Verify domain, hosting, GitHub Pages configuration, analytics and Search Console.
- Record baseline: users, sessions, organic clicks/impressions, engaged sessions, social reach, email signups, enquiries, revenue and costs.
- Remove or clearly label prototype/sample metrics. Never display sample data as live performance.

### Days 4–7: establish the offer
- Publish the editorial standards and corrections/contact route.
- Build a one-page sponsor/commissioned-research offer and enquiry form using only already available free features.
- Prepare a list of 30 relevant local prospective sponsors / research buyers using public business information. No bulk unsolicited messaging; use relevant, personalised outreach and respect opt-outs.

### Days 8–14: launch useful content
- Publish 3–5 high-quality, source-linked pilot pieces or explainers.
- Create short social adaptations from each approved article.
- Distribute via existing social accounts and relevant community channels without spam.
- Add a clear newsletter signup only if a free, compliant tool is already available; otherwise collect no personal data until a suitable consent-based mechanism exists.

### Days 15–21: validate willingness to pay
- Contact a small, carefully selected set of potential buyers with a specific offer.
- Conduct discovery conversations; record needs, objections, budget signals and follow-up.
- Offer a paid pilot brief with scope, deliverables, deadline and fee stated upfront.
- Do not fabricate case studies, audience numbers, reach, or endorsements.

### Days 22–30: evaluate and iterate
- Review which topics attract search/social interest and meaningful enquiries.
- Improve the strongest content and conversion path.
- Report actual collected revenue, qualified pipeline, costs (must remain ₹0 incremental), and next experiments.
- Continue only what demonstrates audience value or buyer demand.

## Weekly founder report (one short review)
1. Verified website users and sessions (source + date range)
2. Search Console clicks and impressions
3. Social reach, engagement, profile visits and link clicks
4. Email subscribers with valid consent
5. Qualified sponsor / research enquiries
6. Proposals, won deals, invoiced revenue, collected revenue
7. Operating spend (must be ₹0 incremental)
8. Work completed, risks, next week's priorities

## Guardrails
- No spending without explicit founder approval; default is no.
- No production domain/site replacement before a verified backup and deployment audit.
- No automated publication of sensitive or potentially defamatory claims.
- No scraped personal data, spam, fake engagement, bought followers, or misleading SEO pages.
- No invented analytics, audience figures, customer logos, revenue, or testimonials.
- No API keys or secrets in Git, client-side code, issues, or chat.
- Do not promise 10,000 DAU or a revenue figure by a deadline. Use experiments and observed conversion data.

## Definition of progress
Progress means verified audience growth, repeat readership, opt-in subscribers, qualified buyer conversations, paid pilots, and collected revenue—not merely more posts or an attractive dashboard.
